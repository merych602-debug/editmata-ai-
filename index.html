HOW IT WORKS

From raw clip to timed meme in 7 steps 🎬

No editing skills needed. Follow the flow once and you'll get it — each step below is exactly what happens in the Studio.

Step 1📤 Upload your video

Drop any MP4/WebM clip. It never leaves your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server in this demo.

Step 2🧠 AI reads the scenes

Hit Analyze: the AI detects scene cuts, transcribes dialogue, and scores the emotion + energy of every part.

Step 3⭐ Key moments found

Funny mistakes, awkward silences, hype wins, plot twists — each flagged with its exact timestamp and a 🔥 score.

Step 4🎭 Get meme picks

For every moment you get matching memes, stickers, GIFs, reactions & sound effects. 🔥 = trending right now.

Step 5👆 Pick one you like

Click any suggestion — or grab one from the 🤳 Social feed, Library, or search by mood ("puppy" finds Doge!).

Step 6⏱️ Auto-sync timing

EditMate suggests the best timestamp to drop it — snapped to the reaction peak, e.g. mistake at 00:18 → pop at 00:18.2.

Step 7👀 Preview & publish

Watch it fire on the timeline, tweak if needed, then render in 16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1 and share to any app. That's the whole trick: right element, right moment.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

New here? Start here 💡

📤 Where does my video go when I upload it?

Nowhere. Your file stays inside your own browser — this demo has no video server. Pick the sample clip if you just want to explore.

🧠 Is it real AI?

This demo simulates the analysis (scene cuts, transcription, emotion scores) so you can test the full flow today. A production version would plug in real video-AI models behind the same buttons.

⏱️ How does auto-sync pick the timestamp?

It takes the moment's timestamp and nudges it to the reaction peak (+0.2s), dialogue pause and beat — so the meme lands on the funny, not before it. You can scrub the timeline to fine-tune.

🤳 Where do Social memes come from?

🖼️ Templates load from Imgflip's trending API; the r/memes, r/dankmemes and r/wholesome tabs stream fresh Reddit posts (NSFW filtered). Categories and % match scores are computed in your browser.

🆓 What's free vs Premium?

Free: 3 meme placements per video, core library, watermarked preview. Premium (₹799/mo): unlimited placements, auto-sync + beat snapping, full marketplace, 4K no watermark. See Pricing.

🎬 I placed a meme — now what?

It appears as a marker on the timeline and pops over your video during preview. Export the timeline as JSON/EDL, or render the final video (16:9 YouTube, 9:16 Reels/Shorts/TikTok, 1:1 feed) and share it anywhere from the publish panel.
